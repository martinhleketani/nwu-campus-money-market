 NWU Campus Money Market

 🛒 About the Project

NWU Campus Money Market is a secure online marketplace designed specifically for North-West University (NWU) students.

The platform allows verified NWU students to buy and sell books, phones, laptops, electronics, clothing, furniture, accessories, study materials, and other student-related items within the university community.

---
👥 Main Users

 🛍️ Buyer

Buyers can:

- Browse and search for products
- View product details and prices
- Filter products by category, campus, location, and price
- Contact sellers
- Purchase items
- View order history
- Rate and review sellers
- Report suspicious listings

 🏪 Seller

Sellers can:

- Create and manage product listings
- Upload product pictures
- Set prices
- Select product categories
- Specify product condition
- Add campus and pickup locations
- Manage orders
- Communicate with buyers
- View sales and transaction history

 👨‍💼 Administrator

The administrator oversees the entire platform and can:

- Manage buyers and sellers
- Manage product listings
- Monitor transactions
- Manage reports
- View total registered students
- View total buyers and sellers
- Monitor total sales
- View transaction values
- View products by category
- View users by campus
- View sales statistics
- Generate graphs and reports

 📍 Location

The system will allow sellers to provide their campus and a suitable pickup location.

Buyers will be able to search for products based on campus and location.

The system will support the North-West University campuses, including:

- Potchefstroom
- Mahikeng
- Vanderbijlpark

User privacy will be considered when handling location information. Exact private residential locations should not be publicly displayed.

🔐 Student Access

The marketplace is designed specifically for verified North-West University students.

Users will be required to provide appropriate NWU student information when registering.

The production system should use an authorized university verification or authentication method.


⭐ Ratings and Reviews

Buyers can rate sellers after completing transactions.

The rating system can help students make informed decisions when interacting with other users.


 🚨 Reporting System

Users can report:

- Suspicious listings
- Possible scams
- Incorrect product information
- Inappropriate content
- Suspicious user activity
- Other marketplace problems

Administrators can review and manage submitted reports.


 📊 Administrator Analytics

The administrator dashboard will provide graphs and statistics including:

- Total students
- Total buyers
- Total sellers
- Active users
- Total products
- Total transactions
- Total transaction value
- Products by category
- Users by campus
- Sales over time
- Reported listings


 🛠️ Technologies

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Node.js
- Express.js
- Database
- Chart.js
- Mapping and location services


 🔒 Security

The system will include security measures to protect:

- Student information
- User accounts
- Passwords
- Transactions
- Product information
- Location information

Role-based access will ensure that buyers, sellers, and administrators have access only to the functions appropriate to their roles.

---

 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of NWU Campus Money Market is to create a professional, secure, and convenient marketplace where verified NWU students can buy and sell products within the university community.
